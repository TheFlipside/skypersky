# Privacy & Security Policy for Skypersky Antivirus

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** May 2026

We believe that security should never come at the cost of privacy. As a free and open-source software (FOSS) project, our commitment to transparency is absolute.

---

## 1. No Data Collection

Skypersky does **not** collect, store, or transmit any user information or data. 

* **No Personal Info:** We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact details.
* **No Usage Data:** We do not track how you use the application.
* **No Telemetry:** The software does not send "heartbeat" signals or usage statistics back to any servers.
* **No File Uploads:** Suspicious files are analyzed locally on your machine. They are never uploaded to a cloud for analysis.

## 2. Local-Only Processing

All scanning, detection, and quarantine actions are performed locally on your device. Skypersky operates within your local environment and does not require an account or internet connection to function (except for updates of virus definitions).

## 3. Transparency & Open Source

Because Skypersky is open-source, our code is available for public audit. Anyone can inspect the source code to verify that no data-collection mechanisms or backdoors exist. We encourage the community to review our codebase to ensure we are living up to our privacy promises.

## 4. Security Practices

* **Integrity:** We provide checksums (hashes) for all official releases to ensure the software has not been tampered with.
* **Minimal Permissions:** Skypersky requests only the system permissions necessary to scan files and protect your system.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Skypersky does not include third-party trackers, advertisements, or analytics engines. 

## 6. Contact

Since we do not collect your data, we have no way to contact you. If you have questions regarding this policy or the software, please visit our official repository.

---

*Protecting your PC, respecting your privacy.*
